ToCo-Split: Topology-Constrained Communication for Efficient Split Learning in Edge Intelligence

Authors: Nasif Fahmid Prangon and Jie Wu

Conference: 23rd IEEE International Conference on Mobile Ad-Hoc and Smart Systems (MASS 2026)

Abstract: We propose ToCo-Split, a topology-aware split learning framework that jointly considers cut-layer selection, distribution-aware client clustering, and structured client-side communication. The framework uses topology-constrained gradient mixing to improve learning efficiency under heterogeneous and non-IID edge environments while balancing communication and computation costs.

FedBaS: Bandwidth-Aware Feature Selection Strategies for Efficient Federated Distillation

Authors: Nasif Fahmid Prangon and Jie Wu

Conference: 2026 IEEE 104th Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC2026-Fall)

Abstract: FedBaS is a bandwidth-aware feature selection framework for federated distillation that reduces communication overhead while preserving model performance. The framework compares FedAvg with logit- and feature-based distillation and introduces variance-, weight-, and mutual information-based feature selection strategies for efficient knowledge sharing in bandwidth-limited edge and wireless networks.

Beyond the Teacher–Student Paradigm: The Role of Network Structure in Knowledge Distillation

Authors: Nasif Fahmid Prangon and Jie Wu

Conference:International Conference on Computing, Networking and Communications (ICNC), 2026

Abstract: In this work, we go beyond the classic one-teacher–one-student setup and study how network topology itself shapes knowledge propagation in multi-student distillation. By analyzing different structures (e.g., chains, rings, hypercubes), we show that who learns from whom can be just as important as what is being learned—especially in edge and distributed learning environments.

Authors: Nasif Fahmid Prangon, Abdalaziz Sawwan, and Jie Wu

Conference: International Conference on Computing, Networking and Communications (ICNC), 2025

Abstract: We propose a resource-aware task allocation strategy that maximizes processing efficiency in energy-constrained edge networks by jointly considering processing capacity, communication delay, and node energy budget.
