Profile

Overview

I am a PhD researcher in Computer and Information Sciences at Temple University, working on edge intelligence, distributed learning systems, knowledge distillation, federated learning, and network-aware machine learning.

My research focuses on how network structure and communication constraints influence learning efficiency in distributed edge AI systems.

Before joining Temple, I worked in telecom core network operations, roaming network planning, and international wholesale services, giving me practical experience in large-scale network infrastructure, traffic analysis, capacity planning, and system reliability.
