Research

Research Interests

My work focuses on the interaction between distributed artificial intelligence and the networks that support it, with an emphasis on communication efficiency, heterogeneity, and intelligent coordination.

01

Distributed & Edge Intelligence

Designing scalable learning systems that operate across distributed and resource-constrained edge environments.

02

Knowledge Distillation

Studying communication-efficient knowledge sharing between heterogeneous learning models in networked systems.

03

Intelligent Cloud Networking

Exploring network-aware intelligence for improving resource allocation, coordination, and performance in cloud and edge systems.

About

Research informed by both academia and industry.

Before beginning my PhD, I worked in the telecommunications industry across technical and strategic roles at Novotel Limited, Robi Axiata Limited, and Axiata Group Wholesale. My work included core network operations, system architecture, roaming service integration, and network optimization.

At Temple University, my research focuses on intelligent cloud networking, topology-aware knowledge distillation, distributed learning, and edge intelligence. I am particularly interested in understanding how network structure and communication constraints can be incorporated directly into the design of distributed learning systems.

This combination of practical telecommunications experience and research in networked computing shapes my broader goal: building distributed intelligent systems that are efficient, scalable, and aware of the networks on which they operate.
